## 1. Problem
In Rwanda, financial exclusion and poverty are interlinked and geographically concentrated. While financial inclusion reached 96% and formal inclusion 92% in 2024 (FinScope 2024), approximately 316,000 adults (4%) remain completely excluded — overwhelmingly rural, elderly, low-education and female-headed households. National poverty fell to 27.4% (EICV7 2023/24), but 41% of VUP beneficiary households remain in poverty, indicating that social protection reduces immediate hardship without reliably breaking the poverty cycle.

## 2. Solution
Finova is an integrated analytical dashboard that combines financial inclusion (FinScope 2024), poverty dynamics (EICV7 2023/24), and social protection outcomes (VUP Thematic Report 2023/24) in one tool. It provides:
- A national overview of key poverty and inclusion indicators
- A district vulnerability map ranking districts by poverty rate
- A VUP beneficiary breakdown by component and poverty status
- Policy recommendations grounded in the data

## 3. Alignment with NST2 and Vision 2050
Finova directly supports NST2 (2024–2029) priorities on poverty reduction, social protection coverage, financial sector deepening, and evidence-based public service delivery. Vision 2050 human development and inclusive growth targets are addressed through a digitally deployed, data-driven dashboard.
